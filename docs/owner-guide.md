# Mira for business owners: your first 15 minutes

Mira answers your customers' questions on your website and WhatsApp, using only what you tell it about your business. You do not need to be technical.

## Set up (once)

1. **Sign up** and name your business.
2. **Import your website** (Settings > Import from website). Mira reads your products, prices and hours so you do not retype them. Check what it found on the Review page and fix anything wrong.
3. **Add the chat to your website** (Settings > Add widget): copy the snippet and paste it on your site.
4. **Try it.** Ask it a few questions the way a customer would. If an answer is wrong, the fix is almost always a missing or wrong fact in your business information.

## Keeping it accurate

Mira only knows what is in your business information, so out-of-date prices mean wrong answers. On the **Inventory** page, type changes in plain words, for example:

- "Mark the red dress as sold out"
- "Change the price of braids to 15000"
- "We close at 5pm on Fridays"
- "Add an FAQ: do you deliver? Yes, within Lagos"

Mira shows what it understood and asks you to confirm before it changes anything.

## Orders

When a customer says they want to buy something, Mira repeats the order back and, once they say yes, records it on the **Orders** page. **It does not take payment and it does not deliver anything.** Each order waits for you to confirm it, so check the Orders page. You can also add an order by hand, and you can switch automatic recording off in Settings.

## When a customer needs a person

If a customer asks for a human, or Mira is not able to help, the conversation is flagged for you and Mira tells the customer someone will follow up. The flag shows in your dashboard (Conversations > needs a person), so check it during business hours, because a customer who is waiting will not be told anything more until you reply. Reply from the Conversations page or on WhatsApp.

## Your plan and bill

You pay a monthly subscription through Paystack. Check `docs/costs.md` for what else may cost money (WhatsApp messages). Your subscription does not renew by itself: you pay again each month. There is no reminder email yet, so note your renewal date. If a payment lapses, Mira switches off for your customers until you pay. You can cancel any time in Settings; your data is deleted 30 days after cancelling, and you can ask for an export first.

## If something looks wrong

Use Custom request in your dashboard. Include the time and what the customer asked.
